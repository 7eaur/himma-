## Root cause and correction

The previous `stopRecording` catch only stopped tracks when `MediaRecorder.stop()` threw. It left recorder callbacks and partial `chunksRef` alive. A delayed `onstop` could still build a nonempty Blob and enable upload of invalid partial audio. The fix nulls all callbacks, invalidates `recorderRef`, drops chunks/blob/object URL, stops tracks and shows retryable error. New Jest regression feeds partial data, forces stop() to throw, then calls the captured late `onstop` and confirms the submission stays disabled without network upload.
