A malformed but HTTP-200 GET/POST could therefore mount a broken question instead of presenting the existing recovery UI. This patch validates render-critical nested structure and keeps the established fail-closed + GET resume path; it does not alter scoring, submission idempotency, V1, video or ElevenLabs.
